How to prepare before building your website

12 Sep 2026

Good preparation saves days of back and forth. Start by writing what you want visitors to do: call you, book, or visit your shop.

Collect your logo, clear photos, a list of services and your contact details. Real photos of your work make a site feel far more trusted than stock images.

Look at two or three websites you like and note what you like about them. This helps me understand your taste quickly.

Finally, decide your budget and your launch date. With these ready, I can plan the work properly and give you an accurate quote.
