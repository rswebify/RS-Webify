How I work

A simple, clear process so you always know what happens next.

  1. Discovery call

    I ask about your business, your customers and what you want the website to do.

  2. Plan and quote

    I list the pages and features, then share a clear price and timeline before any work starts.

  3. Design mockup

    I show you how the website will look. I adjust it until you are fully happy.

  4. Development

    I build the approved design with clean code that is fast and works on every screen.

  5. Testing and launch

    I test on phones, tablets and computers, set up your domain and hosting, then go live.

  6. Support

    After launch I stay available for updates, fixes and new features whenever you need them.
